# Data Availability

The public repository contains aggregate Primary-4 results only. It excludes the prompt-level records, model responses, judge outputs, and human annotation material used during the study.

To reproduce the full pipeline, obtain MT-Bench, AlpacaEval, and WildBench directly from their official sources and follow the respective licenses and access requirements. Download model weights directly from their publishers. Do not commit access tokens, model-weight files, or annotator information to a fork of this repository.

The aggregate files are sufficient to inspect the reported family-preference score, but they are not a substitute for a full inference rerun.
